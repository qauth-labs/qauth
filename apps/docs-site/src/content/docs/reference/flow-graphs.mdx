QAuth's flows are written as semantic graphs in delurio's `delurio-graph/0` format
([ADR-021](/reference/records/adr/021-flows-and-ui-as-semantic-graphs/)). A graph is the
specification of a flow: its states, every way it ends, and the guarantees it keeps. delurio
renders each one as a page with its diagram, served under `/graphs/` (the links are in the list
below; the pages are generated at build time, so they are not content pages of this site).

**None of these graphs is approved yet.** Each one carries a status:

- `captured` records what QAuth does today;
- `proposed` is a design under review.

The YAML in [`graphs/` on GitHub](https://github.com/qauth-labs/qauth/tree/main/graphs) is the
source of record. A rendered page is a view of it and says so at its top; read a graph's status
and history on GitHub. The graphs are outside QAuth's 1.0 stability promise (ADR-021 Decision 6).

Every rendered page also has its diagram as Mermaid source, at `flow.mmd` next to the page. The
same list, with links for language models, is in `/llms.txt`.
